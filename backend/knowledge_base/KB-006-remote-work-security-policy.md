# Remote Work Security Policy

> **Simulated content.** Fictional sample documentation for a demonstration
> system.

## 1. Scope

Applies to all remote and hybrid working, including home offices, co-working
spaces, hotels, airports and customer sites.

## 2. Network requirements

* Always use the **corporate VPN** when accessing internal systems from a
  non-corporate network.
* Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN. If the VPN client cannot connect, use
  a corporate mobile hotspot instead of connecting unprotected.
* Do not disable the VPN client, its kill switch, or the endpoint protection
  agent to "make things faster".
* Do not connect company devices to an unknown or open wireless network.
* Home routers must use WPA2 or WPA3 with a password that is not the factory
  default.

## 3. Device requirements

* Work only on **company-managed devices** for company data. Personal devices
  may be used for email and calendar only through the approved mobile
  application with mobile device management enrolled.
* Keep the operating system, browser and business applications up to date;
  install updates within 7 days of release.
* Full-disk encryption must remain enabled — never disable BitLocker, FileVault
  or the equivalent.
* Do not install unapproved software, browser extensions or "productivity"
  utilities on a managed device.
* Lock the screen whenever you step away, and configure automatic lock after
  5 minutes of inactivity.

## 4. Physical environment

* Position the screen so it is not readable by others in a public place; use a
  privacy filter when travelling.
* Do not discuss confidential matters where you can be overheard.
* Never leave a device visible in a vehicle. Use the boot, not the passenger
  seat.
* Secure paper notes containing confidential information; shred them when no
  longer needed.
* Do not let family members or visitors use a company device.

## 5. Meetings and screen sharing

* Verify who is present before sharing a screen; check the participant list for
  unexpected guests.
* Share a single application window rather than the whole desktop.
* Close confidential documents, chat windows and notification previews before
  sharing.
* Use the "waiting room" or a meeting password for calls that discuss
  confidential or personal data.
* Do not record a meeting containing confidential data without the consent of
  all participants and a documented business reason.

## 6. Data handling

* Store company data only in approved company storage — never on a personal
  cloud drive or a USB stick.
* Do not print confidential documents at home unless it is unavoidable; if you
  must, retrieve the printout immediately and shred it afterwards.
* Do not forward company documents to a personal email address, including to
  yourself, for convenience.
* Classify and handle information according to the Data Leakage Prevention
  Policy.

## 7. Reporting

Report immediately if:

* a device is lost or stolen;
* you suspect your credentials were exposed;
* you connected from a network you now believe was unsafe;
* someone else used your device.

See the Security Contact Guide for how to reach the security team outside
business hours.

## 8. Compliance

Remote work is not a relaxation of security requirements. The same access
control, data handling and reporting obligations apply wherever you work.
